This cookie policy explains the cookies and similar browser storage technologies used on the **QR Zaman** website (qrzaman.com.tr) and in the web management panel (app.qrzaman.com.tr), what they are used for and how you can manage your preferences. Details on personal data processing are in the [Data Protection Notice](/en/legal/data-protection-notice).

## What is a cookie?

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. In a similar way, the browser's local storage (localStorage) lets a site remember your preferences on your device.

## What the website uses

The QR Zaman website **does not use advertising, marketing or tracking cookies** and does not follow you across other sites.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `theme` | Browser local storage (essential) | Remembers your light, dark or system theme preference | Until you delete it |

Forms (demo request, contact, partner application, account deletion) work without cookies. To prevent abuse, your IP address is held briefly in server memory when you submit a form; it is not written to your browser.

## What the panel uses

The web management panel uses only **essential cookies** needed to keep your session secure. You cannot sign in to the panel without them. They are not used for advertising or tracking and become invalid when you sign out or the session expires.

## Analytics cookies

If an analytics tool is used on the website for visitor statistics, analytics cookies are enabled **only with your explicit consent**. Until you consent, no such cookies are written to your browser, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Any tool and cookies used will be listed in the table above.

## Managing your preferences

You can view, delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, you may not be able to sign in to the panel. If you delete your theme preference, the site uses your device's light/dark setting.
