## Data controller and data processor

QR Zaman is a service that helps businesses manage employee attendance (time and attendance), timesheets, leave, salary advances, payroll and customer (CRM) processes. User accounts in the panel and the mobile app are created by the organisation you work for ("your Employer"). Accounts cannot be opened from the mobile app on your own.

- **Your Employer** determines the purposes and means of processing your employee, personnel file, payroll, leave and clock-in/out data, and is therefore the **data controller** for that data.
- **{LEGAL_ENTITY.title}** ("QR Zaman") processes that data on behalf of and on the instructions of your Employer as the **data processor**.
- For website forms (demo request, contact, partner application, account deletion request) and for records relating to account security, device verification and the technical delivery of the service, QR Zaman acts as the **data controller**.

## Purposes of processing

- Fulfilling obligations arising from employment contracts
- Keeping clock-in/out records and calculating timesheets and working hours
- Running salary, payroll, advance, deduction and additional payment processes
- Creating and approving leave and advance requests
- Preventing fake or proxy clock-ins through location and device verification
- Managing user accounts, authorisation and information security
- Responding to your demo, contact and partner applications and sending you quotes and information
- Assessing and completing account and data deletion requests
- Meeting legal obligations under labour, social security and tax legislation (Labour Law No. 4857, Social Insurance and General Health Insurance Law No. 5510, Occupational Health and Safety Law No. 6331, tax legislation)
- Providing information to authorised public bodies and protecting rights in potential disputes
- Delivering the service technically and fixing errors

## Legal grounds

Your personal data is processed on the following grounds under Article 5(2) of the KVKK:

- (a) it is expressly provided for by law, and (ç) it is necessary for the data controller to fulfil a legal obligation,
- (c) it is directly related to the conclusion or performance of a contract (employment contract, service contract, or the quote and demo request you submit through a form),
- (e) it is necessary for the establishment, exercise or protection of a right,
- (f) it is necessary for the legitimate interests of the data controller, provided that it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (for example verifying clock-in records by location and device, information security, preventing form abuse).

**Blood type**, a special category of personal data, is processed under Article 6(3) of the KVKK to meet legal obligations in occupational health and safety and social security, with the adequate safeguards set by the Personal Data Protection Board.

## How we collect data

Your personal data is collected electronically when:

- your Employer or users authorised by your Employer enter it into the QR Zaman web management panel,
- you sign in to the mobile app, scan a QR code or create a leave or advance request,
- location and device data is captured automatically from your device at the moment of a QR scan,
- you fill in a form on the website, together with the IP address your browser sends when submitting the form.

Your data is not shared with third parties for marketing, advertising or profiling. Our servers are located in {LEGAL_ENTITY.serverLocation.en}; your personal data is not transferred abroad.

## Retention periods

- Personnel file, payroll, timesheet and clock-in/out records: for the duration of employment and, after it ends, for the periods required by labour and social security legislation and general limitation periods (generally 10 years),
- Mobile app account and device registration: while your account is active; deleted when the account is closed or your deletion request is completed,
- Website form records: up to 2 years after your request is completed,
- Transaction security logs: 2 years under applicable legislation and information security requirements.

Data whose retention period has expired is deleted, destroyed or anonymised under the Regulation on the Deletion, Destruction or Anonymisation of Personal Data.

## Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to:

- learn whether your personal data is processed and request information if it is,
- learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose,
- know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred,
- request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
- request deletion or destruction under the conditions in Article 7 of the KVKK,
- request that corrections, deletions and destructions be notified to third parties it was transferred to,
- object to an outcome against you arising solely from automated analysis,
- claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.
